minSolo mutes or solos individual apps' audio from the menu bar, with zero
added latency for everything you can still hear.

This installs minSolo.app in /Applications. On first use macOS asks for
System Audio Recording permission: minSolo uses Core Audio process taps to
drop a muted app out of the mix, and the tapped audio is discarded at once.
Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

Requires macOS 14.2 or later.
